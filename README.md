## 🌟 Executive Overview
**Aaranya Bakehouse** is a proposed boutique artisanal home bakery specializing in handcrafted celebration cakes, gourmet stuffed cookies, artisan cupcake platters, and curated festive dessert gift boxes. 

The website is engineered to provide an engaging, customer-centric digital storefront with a transparent enquiry-based ordering model, interactive custom cake estimation calculator, rich on-page SEO, and a comprehensive POEM (Paid, Owned, Earned Media) digital marketing integration.

---

## 🚀 Key Website Features
- **Responsive Multi-Page Architecture:** Semantic HTML5 structure across `Home`, `About Us`, `Products / Services`, and `Contact Us`.
- **Interactive Price Estimator:** Real-time JavaScript calculator for custom cake sizing, flavours, and tier arrangements.
- **Client-Side Form Validation:** Real-time field feedback, event date selection, and instant WhatsApp chat bridging.
- **Visual Design & Branding:** Warm artisan colour palette (Cocoa `#3A2321`, Terracotta `#C26343`, Cream `#FFFDF9`), serif display typography (`Playfair Display`), and curated high-resolution photography.
- **SEO & Structured Data:** Open Graph social tags, Twitter cards, and Schema.org `Bakery` / `LocalBusiness` JSON-LD structured data.
- **POEM Integration:** Digital community feed preview (`#AaranyaCelebrations`), and lead magnet newsletter subscription (10% discount promo).
