Our Origins

Why Aaranya Bakehouse Exists

Founded by Himani Gupta as part of the Digital Innovation in Business initiative, Aaranya Bakehouse represents the modern evolution of the boutique home bakery. In a market flooded with commercial mass-produced cakes made with artificial syrups and vegetable fats, Aaranya was conceived as a mindful sanctuary for real pastry craft.

The name Aaranya evokes purity, organic abundance, and grounding tranquility. We believe that cakes shouldn’t just be decorative sugar sculptures — they must be deeply comforting culinary delights with balanced sweetness, luscious textures, and authentic flavors.

Whether it's an intimate first birthday smash cake, a delicate box of floral cupcakes, or celebratory wedding anniversary bakes, every order is treated as a bespoke personal collaboration.

Core Values

Our Baking Philosophy

The four unwavering standards that guide every recipe in our kitchen.

Small-Batch Precision

We limit our daily bookings to ensure each cake receives dedicated artisan attention, perfect crumb structure, and sharp edges.

100% Honest Ingredients

Real butter, whole eggs or premium yogurt substitutes, Madagascar vanilla beans, and 54.5% Callebaut Belgian dark chocolate.

Zero Artificial Fillers

We do not use premixes, artificial preservatives, or excessive food colors. Our fillings are made of real fruit reductions and silky ganaches.

Responsible Packaging

All dessert hampers and cake carriers use FSC-certified biodegradable board, reusable fabrics, and zero single-use plastics.

Hygiene & Safety

Immaculate Home Studio Standards

Operating as a boutique home bakery requires an even higher standard of cleanliness and allergen awareness than traditional retail outlets:

  • ✔
    Dedicated Pastry Prep Station: Sanitized stainless steel surfaces, temperature-controlled dough cooling, and dedicated baking appliances.
  • ✔
    Strict Allergen Segregation: Distinct color-coded utensils for 100% vegetarian (eggless) and nut-sensitive preparations.
  • ✔
    Freshness Lock: Baked within 12 hours of delivery time to ensure peak moisture and textural integrity.
